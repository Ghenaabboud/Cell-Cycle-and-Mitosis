      Before a cell moves on, it stops at a checkpoint and checks that everything is OK. If something is wrong, it stops and tries to fix the problem. Some cells, like most nerve cells, leave the cycle and rest in G0.

      G1 checkpointBefore copying the DNA
      • Is the cell big enough?
      • Are there enough nutrients?
      • Has it got a signal to divide?
      • Is the DNA undamaged?
      G2 checkpointBefore mitosis
      • Has all the DNA been copied?
      • Is the copied DNA undamaged?
