    Compare

    Mitosis vs Meiosis

    Mitosis

    • 1 division → 2 cells
    • Each cell is diploid (2n): 4 chromosomes here, 46 in you
    • Cells are identical to the parent
    • For growth and repair

    Meiosis

    • 2 divisions → 4 cells
    • Each cell is haploid (n): 2 chromosomes here, 23 in you
    • Cells are all different
    • Makes gametes (sperm and eggs) in testes and ovaries
